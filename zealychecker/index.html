FAQ

Who can use the LIFT Ecosystem?

Whether you're looking to build and monetize your own AI agents or just want to earn passively by turning on DataBridge and sharing unused bandwidth—there’s a way for everyone to participate in the LIFT ecosystem.

How are node operators rewarded?

As users pay $LIFT to access data extracted by the network, those tokens are distributed to node operators who power the system. The more valuable your contribution at any given time, the more $LIFT you earn.

What was the Genesis Node Sale?

The Genesis Node Sale marked the launch of LIFT’s decentralized network. It offered early supporters the chance to operate nodes—and sold out in under 24 hours.

How can I monetize my data on LIFT?

There are four main ways to earn on LIFT:

‍

  • Operate a node
  • Train and deploy AI agents
  • Activate DataBridge to share idle bandwidth
  • Run AI Machines as part of the network’s modular node system

Is my data secure on LIFT?

Yes. LIFT uses a decentralized edge network to process and store data—removing single points of failure. Privacy is further protected through zkEVM technology that ensures sensitive data stays private.

How do I get started with LIFT?

Jump into our Discord to meet the community and get started. Whether you're setting up a node or just exploring, there’s always someone there to help.

What are the hardware requirements to run a node?

LIFT’s modular design means different components have different needs:

‍

  • AI Module: Requires decent compute power
  • ZK Module: Can run on most standard machines
  • Storage Module: Needs available disk space
  • Coordination Module: Lightweight and suitable for most setups

‍

You can choose one or all modules depending on your machine and goals.
